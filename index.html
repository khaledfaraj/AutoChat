About AutoChat

Our story, our vision, and everything you might want to know

Our Story

AutoChat was built around a simple observation: small and growing businesses lose customers every day simply because nobody was available to reply in time. Instant messaging is where customers already are — Telegram and WhatsApp — but staffing a reply desk around the clock isn't realistic for most teams.

So we built a no-code platform that lets anyone launch a working, always-on reply bot in minutes — no developer, no server to maintain, no messages left on read.

Our Vision

We believe automation shouldn't be a luxury reserved for large companies with engineering teams. AutoChat's goal is to give every business — from a one-person shop to a growing team — the same instant, professional, always-available customer experience, without writing a single line of code.

Bot Rules

How every AutoChat bot behaves, on both Telegram and WhatsApp

1. Private conversations only

Bots reply only to direct, one-to-one conversations. They never respond inside group chats, channels, broadcast lists, WhatsApp Status updates, or newsletters — on either Telegram or WhatsApp. This is a deliberate safety rule, not a limitation you can turn off.

2. Real-time replies, not a message queue

Bots respond to new messages while they're actively running. If a bot was turned off or temporarily offline, messages sent during that time will not receive an automatic reply once it comes back online — this prevents customers from getting flooded with delayed responses all at once.

3. You're always in control

Every bot can be turned on or off at any time from the My Bots dashboard. When a bot is off, it stops replying entirely until you switch it back on — no scheduling required.

4. Two response styles

  • Welcome / auto-reply bots — send an automatic greeting or message to anyone who starts a conversation.
  • Menu bots — guide the customer through a numbered menu of options they can navigate by replying with a number.

5. No unsolicited messaging

Bots only ever reply to a message they receive — they never initiate a conversation or send bulk/unsolicited messages to anyone.

These rules apply to every bot created on AutoChat and cannot be disabled by individual bot owners — they exist to keep the platform safe and spam-free for everyone.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

We collect the account information you provide (username, contact details), the bots and settings you create, and the Telegram/WhatsApp session data required to keep your bot connected.

How we protect it

Session data is encrypted at rest, access to your account is protected by a signed session token, and every account's bots and data are isolated from other accounts — no one else can access your bots or messages.

How we use your data

Your data is used solely to operate your bots and your account — to authenticate you, run your bots, and process any subscription you choose to purchase. We do not sell your data to third parties.
