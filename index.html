Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before launching your first AutoChat.

Do I need coding experience to create a bot? +

No — none at all. AutoChat is fully no-code. You can build and launch a working bot using our guided setup without writing a single line of code.

Is my bot available 24/7? +

Yes. Once published, your bot runs around the clock, responding to customers instantly — even while you sleep, and with zero downtime on your end.

Is my data secure? +

Yes. All data is encrypted end-to-end, and every account's bots and information are fully isolated — no one else can ever access your data.

Which platforms are supported? +

Telegram and WhatsApp are fully supported today, with more messaging platforms on the roadmap as the platform grows.

Can I change my bot's responses later? +

Absolutely. You can update your welcome message, AI behavior, and settings anytime from the My Bots dashboard — changes apply instantly.
